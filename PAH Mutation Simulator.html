Questions for the class

  1. Set correct folding to 40% and leave the rest at 100%. Which enzymes disappear from the grid, and why do misfolded and degraded ones look different?
  2. Lower only catalytic activity. The enzyme count stays at 40/40, so why does blood Phe still rise?
  3. Load Mild combined. Three 15% losses leave about 61% of activity and blood Phe just under 120 µmol/L. Why do the losses multiply instead of add?
  4. Which single slider, taken to 5%, does the most damage? Is any one slider worse than the others?

About the model

  • Residual activity is production × folding × stability × catalytic activity.
  • Blood Phe settles where dietary Phe intake equals Michaelis-Menten clearance by the remaining enzyme.
  • The numbers are illustrative. Real Phe levels also depend on diet, BH4 cofactor response and which PAH variants a patient carries.
  • The cell view shows 40 enzymes as a grid for clarity. Real hepatocytes hold far more.
